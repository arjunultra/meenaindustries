Our Story

50 years of colour from Sivakasi

Meena Industries began with a printer's understanding of colour. In the 1960s, our founder Mr. Murukesh Ponnambalam trained in ink technology in Germany for his family's business, National Litho Press, then one of Sivakasi's largest printing houses, led by his father Mr. Ponnambalam.

In 1976 he founded Meena Industries, and in 1977 launched Suncem cement paint, which became a massive hit with painters and dealers. As walls moved from cement paints to distempers and then emulsions, we grew with them: Suncoat in 1983, Sudarshan in 2004, and today a complete range of emulsions, wall putties and primers.

From humble beginnings to our golden jubilee, Mr. Murukesh Ponnambalam still leads the company today.
